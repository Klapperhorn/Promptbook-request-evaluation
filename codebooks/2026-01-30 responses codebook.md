## Code: Insufficient crisis response

Description:

- Claims that crisis management was inadequate, ineffective, too slow, or counterproductive, leaving the crisis unresolved or vulnerabilities unchanged (e.g., prices still high, dependency persists, measures “did nothing”).

Include if: 

- Explicit judgement that actions were insufficient/ineffective (“gar nicht”, “zu wenig Ergebnis”, “wirkt weiterhin bescheiden”).
- Ongoing crisis state used as evidence (still expensive electricity/energy, crisis “still there”).

- Persisting vulnerabilities/dependencies named as unresolved (“Abhängigkeiten bestehen nach wie vor”, not resilient to new shocks).

- Mentions of measures exist but are described as failing to offset burden (“trotz Entlastungspakete … belastet massiv”).

Exclude if:

 - Purely blaming who caused the crisis (use cause codes) without evaluating response adequacy.

- General distrust/insults without a concrete claim of ineffectiveness (unless clearly tied to “no effect / nothing done”).

- Critique focused on wrong direction/negative side effects of specific measures (create/use “harmful response” or “unfair response” if you have those).

- Statements only about personal impact without linking to (in)effectiveness of handling.

Included examples:

- “noch zu wenig Ergebnis …”
- Bisserl was fürs gute Gewissen, mehr nicht
- “für uns ist die Energiekrise noch nicht zu Ende”
- “Strom ist so teuer wie noch nie”
- “Abhängigkeiten bestehen nach wie vor”
- "Eerlijk gezegd heeft het volgens mij weinig invloed gehad"
- “kein Einfluss auf die Bewältigung”
-  Vooral de subsidies zorgen voor de grootste problemen
-  man hat versucht ... aber



## Code: Increase in renewable energy production

Description:
claims that authorities/industry increased (or tried to increase) renewable energy supply or accelerated deployment as a crisis response.

Include if: 
- Mentions “they expanded wind/solar,” “more renewables,” “fast-tracking renewable permits,” “renewable rollout,” “green transition pushed.”

Exclude if: 
- Focus is mainly on R&D/innovation rather than deployment (use Accelerating technology development).
- Claims that there was only talk without action

Included examples: “They pushed wind and solar permits through faster to reduce gas dependence.”

## Code: Finding other countries to import energy from

Description:
Respondent perceives a shift to new external suppliers/import routes (gas, oil, LNG, electricity).

Include if: 
- “They bought LNG,” “new contracts with Norway/US/Qatar,” “import diversification,” “new pipeline deals.”

Exclude if: 
- The emphasis is EU collective action/joint procurement framed as cooperation (use European cooperation), though you can double-code if both are clearly present.

Included example: 
- “They replaced Russian gas by importing LNG from the US and Qatar.”
- import van vloeibaar gas uit andere landen

## Code: European cooperation

Description:
Respondent perceives good EU/European-level coordination as part of the response (solidarity, joint procurement, coordinated policy).

Include if:
- “EU coordinated,” “joint buying,” “solidarity mechanism,” “Europe worked together,” “EU-wide plan.”
- Explicitly states countries cooperated better or moved closer together due to the crisis.
- Attributes the successful response to the crisis to the EU.
- Mentions good collaboration even if acknowledging simultaneous tensions.

Exclude if:
- Only mentions “importing from other countries” without cooperation framing.
Included
- Statements emphasizing failed/planless EU cooperation

Examples:
“The EU coordinated purchases and tried to act together on energy security.”
- “Die Länder haben enger zusammengearbeitet, um das gemeinsam zu bewältigen”
- “...ich ga er vanuit dat er wel is samengewerkt.”
- “...die Zusammenarbeit zwischen landen ist verbessert.”

## Code: Energy savings of households and industry

Description:  
States the crisis led people (self/household/citizens) or industry to reduce energy use or adopt energy-saving practices, whether voluntary (efficiency) or forced (rationing, heating less).

Inclusion criteria:

- Mentions concrete actions to save energy: using less electricity, heating less, adopting saving routines.
    
- Mentions inability to reduce further but still frames the situation in terms of energy-saving constraints.
- Mentions that they asked people to save energy,” “lower thermostat,” “campaigns”

Exclusion criteria:

- Describes increasing prices without describing energy-saving behavior
    
- Pure learning/awareness without behavior change


Included examples:

- “Ich habe deutlich weniger Geld und spare jetzt fast den ganzen Strom ein…”
- obwohl der Verbrauch sich gesenkt hat
- “Die Nebenkosten sind zu hoch, und ich kann sie nicht weiter senken.”
- “...den ganzen Winter wieder frieren muss…”
- "They ran campaigns telling households to reduce heating and businesses to cut consumption."

## Code: Efforts to reduce consumer prices

Description:
- Respondent perceives actions intended to lower household/consumer energy bills or limit price increases.
  
Include if: 
- “price cap,” “subsidies,” “tax cuts/VAT reduction,” “rebates,” “support payments,” “bill relief,” “compensation scheme,” “regulating prices.”
- Mentions efforts to lower the burden for low-income households
- Mentions efforts to fight inflation and increased prices.

Exclude if: 
- Focus is about securing supply (imports/storage) without consumer price relief.

Example:
- "een prijsplafond invoeren zodat de kosten te overzien blijven"
- Zuschuss für Studenten
- Mit mehr Staatsschulden sind wir bisher halbwegs gut durchgekommen
- Huishoudens met een laag inkomen kregen steun uit een noodfonds, en de energiebelasting werd een tijdje verlaagd

## Code: Timely filling of energy storage

Description:
Respondent perceives actions to fill gas storage/strategic reserves ahead of winter, build reserves, or mandate storage levels.

Include if: 
- “filled gas storage,” “strategic reserves,” “storage targets,” “stockpiling,” “they secured reserves before winter.”
Exclude if: 
- General “energy security” claims without explicit storage/reserves.
Example:
- “They made sure gas storage was filled in time for winter.”

## Code: Accelerating technology development

Description:
- Respondent perceives investment/effort to develop new technologies or accelerate innovation (not just deploy existing renewables).

Include if: 
- frames innovation efforts as a response to the crisis
- “invested in hydrogen,” “R&D,” “new nuclear/SMRs,” “grid innovation,” “heat pump tech breakthroughs,” “new storage tech,” “innovation funding,” "fusion technology", "smart grid"

Exclude if: 
- Straight deployment of existing wind/solar (use Increase in renewable energy production).
- Pure heat pump rollout without mentioning innovation.

Example:
- “They poured funding into hydrogen and new energy storage technologies.”
