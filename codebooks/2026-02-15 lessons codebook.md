## Code: Accelerate the transition towards renewable energies

Description:  
Recommendations to speed up expansion of renewables (e.g., wind/solar) and related measures to push the energy transition.

Exclude if suggestions:

- focus only on reduction/efficiency
- focus only on grid/storage infrastructure

Included examples:

- dezentraler Ausbau erneuerbarer Energien
- Stärker auf erneuerbare Energien setzen

## Code: Slow down the transition towards renewable energies

Description:  
Recommendations to delay/slow/sequence the ph1ase out of fossil energy, arguing that the pace is too fast or that alternatives/infrastructure are not ready.

Exclude if:

- They criticize renewables but do not recommend slowing down.
- They recommend nuclear power without slowing down the transition

Included examples:

- "Te snel overstappen van fossiele brandstoffen naar puur electrich."
- "Man sollte... nichts abschaffen wenn keine genügenden Alternativen vorhanden sind"
